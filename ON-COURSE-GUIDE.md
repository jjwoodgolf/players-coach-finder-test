# On-Course Guide: Accuracy Test (v0.1)

**Goal:** find out whether phone GPS yardage to the pin is within **±3 yards of your laser on 90% or more of shots**. You need **50+ comparisons** over 3 or more sessions.

---

## One-time setup (5 minutes)

1. **Turn on Precise Location for Safari.**
   Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → **Safari Websites** → "While Using the App" and **Precise Location ON**.
2. **Open the test page in Safari** (the link Claude sends you).
3. **Add it to your Home Screen.** Tap the Share button → **Add to Home Screen** → Add.
   - **From now on, always open it from the Home Screen icon.** The icon and Safari store data separately, so switching between them splits your results.
4. Open the icon and tap **Start**, then tap **Allow** for Motion & Orientation, Camera and Location.

---

## Testing during a normal round

On each hole you test:

1. **At your ball:**
   - Laser the flag and wait for your laser's flag-lock vibration.
   - Tap **Log laser** and type the laser number. The app averages your GPS position for 5 seconds while you type.
   - Tap **Save**.
2. **After you hole out:**
   - Make sure the hole number at the top is right.
   - Tap **Mark pin** and hold the phone **directly over the hole** while standing beside it.
   - Keep still for **15 seconds** until the bar fills.
   - The app fills in the yardage for every shot you logged on that hole.
3. Tap **Next hole →** to move on.

## Faster practice session (optional)

Good for quiet times at the course:

1. Go to a green and tap **Mark pin** over the hole.
2. Walk back to roughly 50, 100, 150 and 200 yards.
3. At each spot, laser the flag and tap **Log laser**.

That's 4 comparisons per hole in a few minutes.

---

## Good test habits

- **Wait for the green GPS chip** (±5 m or better) before logging or marking a pin.
- **Use your laser's flag lock every time.** If you think the laser hit the trees, add a note ("may have hit trees") or delete that shot under Results.
- **Mix it up:** different holes, distances (about 50–250 yards), times of day and weather.
- **The camera and bullseye** are there to practice the aiming feel. In this version the yardage comes from GPS, not the camera. When the phone is steady for about 1 second you'll hear a **ding**, and the screen flashes green.
- **No ding?** Turn off Silent mode, or check Results → Settings → Ding sound.

---

## After every round: send the results

1. Tap **Results** (top right) → **Share CSV**.
2. Choose how to send it:
   - **AirDrop** it to your MacBook, or
   - **Save to Files**, or
   - **Mail** it to yourself.
3. Upload the file in your Claude chat. Claude checks every shot and looks for patterns: GPS quality, certain holes, time of day.

The Results screen also shows live progress: **"Keep going: X of 50,"** then **PASS / BORDERLINE / NOT YET**.

---

## What the colors mean

| Where | Green | Yellow | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| GPS chip | ±5 m or better: good | ±6–10 m: OK | Worse than 10 m, or no signal: wait |
| Results "Diff" column | Within ±3 yards | 4–5 yards off | More than 5 yards off |

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| GPS chip stays red | Step into the open, away from trees and buildings, and wait 30 seconds. Check that Precise Location is ON (setup step 1). |
| "Location is blocked" | Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Safari Websites → While Using + Precise Location ON. Then reopen the app. |
| Bullseye never locks | Motion permission was denied. Tap the bullseye to lock by hand, or delete the Home Screen icon, re-add it and tap Allow. |
| Camera is black | Not needed for this test; everything still works. To fix, allow Camera when asked. |
| Wrong hole number | Use − / + at the top before logging. To correct a mistake, delete the shot or pin under Results. |

**Your data is saved only on this phone.** Export after every round.
